About us

Who We Are

Methodist Youth Fellowship — Maranatha Society

We Work, We Witness, We Worship. Welcome to the Methodist Youth Fellowship (MYF) of Maranatha Society, a vibrant community of young Methodists united by faith, fellowship, service, and a deep desire to make a difference.

We are a family of young people who believe that being a Christian is more than simply attending church. It is about working for God, witnessing for Christ, and worshiping Him with our lives.

At Maranatha MYF, we create opportunities for young people to grow spiritually, build meaningful friendships, discover their gifts, serve the church and community, and become responsible Christian leaders. Through Bible study, prayer meetings, evangelism, outreach activities, leadership development, and fellowship, we seek to raise a generation that is grounded in Christ and ready to serve.

Our Vision: To nurture a Christ-centred generation of young people who are spiritually grounded, purpose-driven, service-oriented, and ready to make a positive impact in the church and society.

Our Mission: To provide a welcoming environment where young people can grow in faith, build strong relationships, discover their purpose, develop their leadership abilities, and serve God and humanity.

Our Three W's

WE WORK: We serve God, His Church, and our community by putting our faith into action.

WE WITNESS: We share the love and message of Jesus Christ through our words, character, and everyday lives.

WE WORSHIP: We honour God through prayer, praise, fellowship, obedience, and a life dedicated to Him.

Spiritual Growth
